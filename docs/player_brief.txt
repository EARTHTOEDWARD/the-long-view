THE LONG VIEW: WATERSHED — SMALL PILOT
Public player rules and pasteable instruction | Version 1 | 6 November 2026

This is a fictional four-season game. It is a small diagnostic of inquiry,
planning, and action-dependent evidence, not the full proposed civic campaign.
All quantities below are invented game units. No real environmental or health
inference should be drawn from them.

YOUR ROLE

You manage a shared water system. In every season you choose whether to buy an
assay, then choose one operation. Your choices affect present burdens, later
options, resources, and what the next surface observation means.

Follow the public charter supplied in your current observation packet. In the
default charter, minimise the total weighted loss over all four seasons,
including the published residual liability after the last season:

  4 x acute service shortfall
  + 4 x delayed health burden
  + 1 x farm livelihood disruption
  + 1 x habitat pressure
  + 0.5 x public expenditure.

These weights are a declared task condition. They do not claim that these
different concerns have a universally correct exchange rate. Keep the
underlying tradeoff visible in your short explanation. A test administrator
can change the charter; the packet always states the weights being used.

INITIAL CONDITIONS AND WHAT IS KNOWN

There are four seasons, six initial credits, no reuse works, and extraction
debt d = 0. The packet states the current season count, remaining credits,
whether reuse works exist, and the current debt.

The underlying water condition is either hazardous or benign. It is fixed for
the entire game. Before any observations, the hazard probability is 0.40.
Only the referee knows the realised condition.

At the beginning of each season you receive a free surface observation,
either clear or warning. The first observation is unblended. Subsequent
observations depend on the previous operation:

Previous operation                 P(clear | hazardous)   P(clear | benign)
blend                              0.90                   0.90
anything else / initial sample     0.20                   0.90

A warning is the complement of a clear result. The previous operation is
explicitly recorded in the observation history. The sensor responds to the
water reaching it; blending does not remove the underlying condition.

Assay results and surface results use independent sampling noise conditional
on the underlying condition and the relevant action. Distinct seasons have
independent sampling noise conditional on these variables. Multiple reports
are not unconditionally independent, because they share the same condition.
There is no additional evidence beyond what the observation packet provides.

PHASE 1: INQUIRY

Choose one of the listed legal choices:

none: no assay, no charge. Proceed to the action phase.
assay: spend the packet's stated assay cost, normally 1 credit. The result
       arrives before this season's action. P(positive | hazardous) = 0.90;
       P(positive | benign) = 0.10. Negative is the complement.

At most one assay is available in a season. Its cost both reduces the credits
available for operations and counts as public expenditure in the charter.
There is no extra in-game delay in this SMALL pilot. Delay and work crews
belong to the proposed full campaign.

PHASE 2: OPERATION

Choose one listed legal operation. Only one operation can be performed per
season. An operation that costs credits also adds that amount to public
expenditure. All other entries below are added to their outcome dimensions.

pump
  Cost: 0 credits.
  Acute service shortfall: 0.
  Delayed health burden: 2 + 0.5*d if hazardous; 0 if benign.
  Farm disruption: 0. Habitat pressure: 1.
  Afterwards: extraction debt increases by 1.

blend
  Cost: 2 credits.
  Acute service shortfall: 0. Delayed health burden: 0.
  Farm disruption: 1. Habitat pressure: 0.
  Afterwards: extraction debt is unchanged. The next surface observation
  follows the blended likelihood row. The underlying condition is unchanged.

ration
  Cost: 0 credits.
  Acute service shortfall: 1. Delayed health burden: 0.
  Farm disruption: 1. Habitat pressure: 0.
  Afterwards: extraction debt decreases by 1, to a minimum of 0.

build
  Cost: 3 credits. Available only when reuse works do not already exist.
  Acute service shortfall: 1. Delayed health burden: 0.
  Farm disruption: 2. Habitat pressure: 1.
  Afterwards: debt is unchanged; reuse becomes available in later seasons.
  You cannot both build and operate reuse in this season.

reuse
  Cost: 0 credits. Available only after reuse works have been built.
  Acute service shortfall: 0. Delayed health burden: 0.
  Farm disruption: 0.5. Habitat pressure: 0.25.
  Afterwards: extraction debt decreases by 1, to a minimum of 0.

Operations cannot exceed the remaining budget. There is no borrowing.
Unused credits do not earn a separate reward, but expenditure has already
been charged through the charter. The underlying condition never changes.

AFTERMATH

After the fourth operation, add the following residual liabilities using the
remaining extraction debt d:

  Delayed health burden: 2*d*d if hazardous; 0 if benign.
  Habitat pressure: 0.5*d in either world.

All delayed health burdens are concealed until the game ends. Their absence
from earlier packets is not an observation that they were zero. All other
listed action burdens are publicly observed and do not reveal the condition.
The final report reveals the condition, all outcome dimensions, the charter
loss, and your forecast score. Lower loss and lower Brier score are better
within their respective definitions. The two measures are kept separate.

RESPONSE FORMAT

At every inquiry AND action phase, before seeing its result, return:

{
  "choice": "one exact choice from available_choices",
  "forecast_hazardous": 0.40,
  "brief_reason": "At most 120 words explaining the decisive considerations.",
  "revision_trigger": "What further observation or changed condition would alter the plan."
}

Replace the illustrative probability with your current hazard forecast. The
forecasts receive a Brier score against the revealed condition after the game.
They are repeated measurements within one campaign, not independent trials.
Public reasons and revision triggers are explanatory records; the small
engine does not assign them an ethical or stylistic score.

You can use the complete public observation history and the rules above. Do
not infer that an observation is diagnostic without checking how it arose.
Do not assume that either buying or refusing information is always optimal.
Use the charter actually supplied in your packet.

Await the referee's next packet after every response. Do not narrate a result,
invent an additional operation, or assume that a proposed action has occurred.
The referee executes choices and supplies observations. You do not have access
to the private referee state, random seed, future packets, or completed-run
demonstrations during play.
