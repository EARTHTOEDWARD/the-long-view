## 0.1.0 — 2026-11-06

Initial public packaging of the Watershed diagnostic kernel.

- Four seasons with inquiry and action phases, a persistent hidden condition, and action-dependent observations.
- Paid assays, five operational choices with prerequisites, and construction that unlocks reuse.
- Separate outcome dimensions, a declared toy charter, and a published terminal liability.
- Exact same-information Bayesian reference with rational belief updates.
- Fourteen semantic tests and 256 recorded scripted games over 64 paired worlds.
- Separate hypothetical and reachable checkpoint diagnostics, with their selection limits recorded.
- Public player brief, longer campaign design, contribution guidance, and source provenance.
- Completed [independent Pro design review](reviews/pro_review_v0_1.md), prepared before inspecting the newly commissioned Astra results.
- Completed [exploratory Astra pilot](experiments/astra_v0_1/README.md): four games over two paired worlds and four separate checkpoint decisions, using the fixed 36-response budget.
- Retained records show zero local regret for all 32 game decisions and four diagnostic decisions; tied optimal actions receive credit. The report retains the lack of masked observations in complete games and makes no model-ranking claim.
- Added a standard-library [replay verifier](experiments/astra_v0_1/verify_pilot.py) and [verified summary](experiments/astra_v0_1/summary.json), with posterior reconstruction error and exposure counts labelled as post-hoc secondary diagnostics.
- Retained both [manual input deviations](experiments/astra_v0_1/protocol_deviations.json): C1 punctuation and B2's discrepant historical expenditure. B2 is not a clean protocol replication; no affected run was discarded or repeated.

The kernel source is unchanged from the initial starter kit. The records distinguish requested model configuration from unavailable attestation and usage telemetry. This entry does not report an LLM ranking or architectural superiority.
